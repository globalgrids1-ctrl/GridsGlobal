# Navigating Beam to Column Connection Design: Common Pitfalls & Fixes

> Steel buildings live or die by their joints. Master beam-to-column connections to avoid costly failures.

## Why Connections Matter

Beams/columns form the skeleton. **Connections** dictate load flow, stability, energy dissipation. Weak designs = deflection, instability, hazards, collapse.

[Structural Steel Connection Design Services](https://gridsglobal-detailing.com/services/connection-design/) ensure pro performance.

## Connection Types

- **Shear (Simple)**: Shear transfer + rotation (fin plates, angles)
- **Moment (Rigid)**: Shear + moments (flange plates, end plates) 
- **Semi-Rigid**: Partial restraint balance

## Top 6 Mistakes

### 1. Inaccurate Load Assumptions
Ignore laterals/seismics → failure.  
**Fix**: Full combos in ETABS.

### 2. Poor Bolt Selection & Spacing
Wrong grade/edges → shear/tear.  
**Fix**: AISC J3 compliance.

### 3. Improper Welding
Small welds/fatigue skip → cracks.  
**Fix**: NDT inspections.

### 4. Ignoring Design Codes
No block shear/prying → unsafe.  
**Fix**: Automated checks.

### 5. Neglecting Seismic Design
No continuity plates → quake fail.  
**Fix**: Strong column–weak beam.

### 6. Detailing Failures
Misalignments → rework/delays.
